# Calendario Studio

> **A bilingual (ES/EN), print-ready calendar builder that runs entirely in your browser.**

Calendario Studio is a lightweight web app for creating clean, customizable calendars and turning them into polished PDFs, PNGs or standalone HTML files — without accounts, servers, or installations.

---

## What is it?

A single-page tool that helps you design a calendar in minutes: pick the month and year, decide how many months to display, add a title and subtitle, drop events on specific days with color tags, and print it — all inside the browser.

No backend, no login, no tracking. Your work autosaves locally and can be exported at any time.

---

## What can you do with it?

- 🗓️ Build calendars for **1, 2 or 3 consecutive months** (single month or full quarter).
- 📅 Choose your **week start** — Monday or Sunday.
- ✏️ Set a **title, subtitle and footer note** for each calendar.
- 🎨 Add **events with color tags** by clicking any day.
- 🖨️ Print or save as **A4 PDF** in portrait or landscape.
- 💾 Export a **standalone HTML** file or a **PNG backup** — and import it back anytime.
- 🌐 Switch between **Spanish and English** with a single click.

---

## Who is it for?

Anyone who needs a tidy, printable monthly calendar without opening a heavy design tool:

- Teams sharing a **quarterly plan** or sprint overview.
- Teachers preparing **class schedules**.
- Freelancers and small studios delivering a **branded calendar** to clients.
- Anyone who wants a **clean A4 sheet** with notes on specific days.
